research compendium** bundling the canonical synthesis report (`README.md`), raw dialogue logs, downloaded research
papers, web HTML dumps, and prototypes.

## The Exploration Standard

Every architectural exploration follows the standard defined in [
`.agents/skills/explorations/SKILL.md`](../../.agents/skills/explorations/SKILL.md):

1. **Dedicated Directory:** `references/explorations/EXP-XXXX-<kebab-case>/`
2. **Canonical Report:** `README.md` containing:
    - Metadata Header Table (`EXP`, `Title`, `Author`, `Status`, `Type`, `Created`, `Updated`, `Related EPs`)
    - `## 1. Abstract & Context`
    - `## 2. Exploration & Options Analysis`
    - `## 3. Architectural Synthesis & Constraint Alignment`
    - `## 4. The Verdict / Actionable Design`
    - `## 5. Related Enhancement Proposals & Bundled Data`
3. **Preserved Empirical Artifacts:** Raw transcripts, downloaded research papers, and web dumps stored in the
   compendium directory.
